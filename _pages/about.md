  Staff ML Research Engineer &bull; LLM Inference Systems, Model Compression &amp; Efficient GenAI
</div>

<p class="intro-lead">
  I am a <strong>Staff ML Research Engineer</strong> at <strong>Qualcomm AI Research</strong> (Toronto), focusing on efficient Generative AI, LLM inference systems, model compression, and hardware-aware kernel optimization. Previously, I worked on neural network compression at <strong>Huawei Noah's Ark Lab</strong> (Montreal) and earned my thesis-based <strong>M.Sc. in Computer Science</strong> from <strong>McGill University</strong>.
</p>

<div class="intro-grid">
  <div class="intro-card">
    <div class="intro-card-title">Generative AI Efficiency &middot; Qualcomm AI Research</div>
    <p class="intro-card-desc">Cross-vocabulary speculative decoding (<em>OmniDraft</em>, <strong>NeurIPS 2025</strong>), recurrent weight-sharing ASR (<strong>NeurIPS 2026</strong>), on-device forward-gradient training (<strong>NeurIPS 2024</strong>), multimodal foundation models, and discrete diffusion LLMs (dLLMs).</p>
  </div>
  <div class="intro-card">
    <div class="intro-card-title">Low-Level Systems &amp; GPU Kernels</div>

  </div>
  <div class="intro-card">
