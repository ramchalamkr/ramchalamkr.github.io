## 🏆 PIEoneer Award Nominee — International Alum of the Year (2025)

Recognized globally as an outstanding York University graduate, shortlisted as a finalist in the prestigious **International Alum of the Year** category at [The PIEoneer Awards](https://thepie.com/news/pieoneer-awards/) — one of the highest honours in international education. This recognition celebrates meaningful contributions to advancing women's education in India and a demonstrated commitment to breaking barriers in cybersecurity.

Honoured also as the **Keynote Speaker** for the 2025 Graduation Ceremony at York University, School of Continuing Studies — speaking to an audience of graduates, faculty, and industry leaders about resilience, career pivots, and the power of lifelong learning.

📄 **[Read the full story — York University SCS](https://continue.yorku.ca/breaking-barriers-in-cybersecurity-yorku-scs-alumna-nominated-for-prestigious-pieoneer-award/)**
