## Overview

As the capstone for my Graduate Certificate in Cybersecurity Operations, I designed a full incident response plan for Big Leaf Enterprise, a small executive consulting and coaching firm. The project covered the entire organization and was delivered as a formal presentation to both the company and university faculty.

---

## Deliverables

- **Incident Response Plan** — a complete written framework covering the full incident lifecycle: preparation, detection, containment, eradication, recovery, and lessons learned
- **Escalation flowcharts** — decision trees for each threat category, mapping who acts, what they do, and when under real incident conditions

---

## Methodology

- **Threat modeling** — identified realistic adversary scenarios relevant to a professional services firm and mapped them to potential impact
- **Containment and recovery procedures** — defined step-by-step procedures for each threat type, including communication protocols and evidence preservation
- **Adversary scenario validation** — tested the plan's logic against realistic attack scenarios to confirm it held up under pressure

## Key Outcomes

- Delivered a complete IR framework that a small organization with no dedicated security team could realistically implement
- Escalation flowcharts gave non-technical staff a clear path to follow during an incident
- Presented successfully to both company leadership and university faculty — translating technical procedures into business-relevant guidance
