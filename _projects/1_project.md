## Project Overview

**Duration:** Feb 2023 – Mar 2023  
**Organization:** Big Leaf Enterprise  
**Role:** Capstone Project Lead

## Objectives

Designed and delivered a comprehensive incident response plan ensuring alignment with local data governance policies and organizational objectives to enhance the organization's cybersecurity posture.

## Key Accomplishments

### Strategic Planning
- Developed end-to-end incident response framework aligned with industry best practices and regulatory requirements
- Ensured compliance with local data governance policies and organizational security objectives
- Created detailed incident classification and escalation procedures

### Risk Assessment
- Conducted comprehensive risk evaluations across the organization's IT infrastructure
- Identified critical assets and potential threat vectors
- Implemented strategies to improve overall cyber resilience

### Stakeholder Communication
- Utilized excellent presentation skills to explain complex cybersecurity concepts to non-technical stakeholders
- Delivered executive-level briefings on incident response procedures and organizational impact
- Ensured cross-functional understanding of roles and responsibilities during security incidents

## Deliverables

- Complete Incident Response Plan documentation
- Risk assessment reports and mitigation strategies
- Executive presentation materials
- Training materials for staff awareness

## Technologies & Frameworks

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework
- Incident Response methodologies
- Risk assessment tools
- Documentation and presentation software

## Impact

Enhanced the organization's ability to detect, respond to, and recover from cybersecurity incidents while ensuring business continuity and regulatory compliance.
